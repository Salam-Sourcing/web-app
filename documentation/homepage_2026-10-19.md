# Homepage implementation — 2026-10-19

Adapted the supplied homepage PDF to the website root and native app home.

## Completed

- Canada-based navy/burgundy hero; buyer/supplier paths; eight sectors with 32
  linked topic searches; published catalog categories; RFQ and supplier actions;
  real catalog previews; process overview; regional links; planned trade events;
  membership links and closing brand statement.
- Website `/categories` supports sector/topic navigation and lists current live
  marketplace categories. `/trade-events` describes proposed concepts and opens
  topic-specific interest emails, without claiming bookings or confirmed venues.
- Native signed-in Discover and guest home share the new presentation. Sector
  taps open native category sheets. Search opens native discovery; RFQ and
  supplier actions open their native flows. Guest intents retain search, region
  and destination through sign-in. New tap controls and Back actions have haptics.
- Existing full search/filter controls remain available. Signed-in web home uses
  authenticated catalog visibility and private cache headers; guests retain the
  anonymous preview. No privileged keys or private listing fields are exposed.
- The two clients share byte-identical `parity/homepage.json`. Sector topics are
  curated query shortcuts; they do not rename or create database taxonomy.
- Uses real published listings and supplier identities, with honest empty/error
  states. PDF example companies, RFQs, verification badges and illustrative
  CAD 495/year pricing are not published as live claims. Existing proposed plans
  remain read-only. Planned events have no confirmed dates or venues.
- Added a 60-second guided process overview with play/pause/replay controls.
- Recorded the actual iPhone 14 simulator home and category sheet as a scrolling
  MP4. Preview media is kept outside the repositories.
